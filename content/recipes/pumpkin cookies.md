## Directions

### Cookies

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
2. In a large bowl, cream together the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar until well blended.
3. Add the egg, vanilla, and pumpkin, and mix until combined.
4. In a separate bowl, mix together the flour, pumpkin pie spice, baking soda, and salt.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
6. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes per batch.
7. Let cool completely before icing.

### Caramel-Like Icing

1. In a small saucepan, melt the butter with the heavy cream and brown sugar, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
2. Remove from heat and gradually stir in confectioners' sugar until the icing reaches your desired sweetness and consistency.
3. Spread over the cooled cookies.

### Cream Cheese Icing (Alternative)

1. Whip the cream cheese with a splash of vanilla.
2. Gradually beat in confectioners' or granulated sugar to taste.
3. Spread over the cooled cookies.
